# Component inventory

Extracted from the two supplied kit sheets on 2026-10-27. These are the components listed on the packaging; actual stock and working condition have not been checked. Quantities are transcribed where printed. A dash means the quantity is not printed and needs to be checked against the physical kit. Similar components in the two kits remain separate entries.
