KINDLE SCRIBE V2 - PRINT THE TEST PARTS FIRST

For just the two revised pen holders, open
Kindle_Scribe_V2_Pen_Holders_Only_XPlus5.3mf. Both are on one plate.

Open Kindle_Scribe_V2_Complete_XPlus5.3mf in QIDI Studio. Plate 1 has
both new 24 mm pen holders plus the two hinge coupons. If the hinges
already passed, print just the holders from this plate. The standalone
Kindle_Scribe_V2_Pen_Cradle_9p4mm_Test.3mf has only the upper holder.

Print 1 of each supplied STL, at 100% scale in millimetres.
Keep their supplied orientations. 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height,
4 walls, 100% infill. Start with supports off and inspect the short bridges.

1. Remove brim, strings and first-layer bulges.
2. Put the single ear of TEST_B between the two outer ears of TEST_A.
3. Check alignment with an M3 x 25 mm screw. The screw must pass freely,
   and the hinge must rotate without spreading or crushing the ears.
   The design has a 3.4 mm bore and 0.5 mm clearance on each side.
4. Lay your Premium Pen in the 9.4 mm opening of the extended part 06. It must
   lift out freely without excessive side-to-side movement; do not force
   it into a tight clip. This is an open cradle, not a spring clamp.
5. Check the new lower ring. Slide
   the pen through from the rear, eraser first. The closed front wall
   must stop it from sliding out while the ring remains easy to use.
6. Reuse parts 05 and 06 in the final assembly if the tests pass.

Do not scale the complete model to correct a small tight hole.
The two hinge coupons are test pieces, not part of the finished stand.
The approximate fully-solid PLA amount is 10.0 g for all four parts,
excluding brim/purge, assuming a material density of 1.24 g/cm3.

Read START_HERE.html in the main V2 package before printing/assembling
all parts. The longer holders and closed ring still need a physical fit test.
